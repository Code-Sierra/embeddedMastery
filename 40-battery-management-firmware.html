BMS Firmware — from one cell to a swap-station fleet

Everything an EV / BMS / charger interviewer (Tesla, Ather, Ola Electric, Battery Smart, Apple battery & power) expects you to explain, taught from scratch. Reference packs: a 16S NMC 30 Ah 2-wheeler pack (≈58 V nominal) and a 16S LFP 3-wheeler pack (≈51 V); reference AFE: TI BQ76952 (3–16 cells), with ADI LTC6811/6813 and NXP MC33771 for bigger, daisy-chained packs. Open the cards in order — each one builds on the previous one.

// 40 — LIVE PACK SIMULATOR

Pack Simulator — cells, protections, SoC estimators, balancing

A configurable series pack runs a ride or a swap-station charge. Each series group has its own capacity, resistance, RC polarisation, hysteresis and temperature. The BMS firmware model measures (with errors), runs protections with thresholds and debounce delays, estimates SoC three ways, balances passively and drives a state machine. Inject faults and watch what the firmware does. Current sign convention: + = charging, − = discharging.
