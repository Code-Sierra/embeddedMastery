// EMBEDDED INTERVIEW MASTERY

Study dashboards, foundations → interview rounds

41 interactive dashboards (2 more coming later), from C and bit manipulation to architecture, Linux, safety, domain topics and the interview rounds themselves. Each has deep theory, a working simulator, debugging scenarios, a Q&A bank and a cheat sheet. Tick a topic when you can answer its whole Q&A bank out loud.

// HOW TO STUDY THIS

  1. Read the Concepts tab top to bottom once; open every collapsed card.
  2. Play with the simulator until you can predict its next step before pressing Step.
  3. Do the scenarios cold. Your first pick is scored; read why the wrong options are wrong.
  4. Answer each Q&A out loud in under 2 minutes before revealing. That is the interview.
  5. Revise from the Cheat Sheet the night before. Work through the tiers in order; for a firmware role, the core path is Tier 1 → Tier 2 → RTOS and Concurrency → the interview rounds in Tier 8.
