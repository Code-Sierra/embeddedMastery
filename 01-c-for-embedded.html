        // 01.4 — DECLARATION DECODER
        
        
        Read Any Declaration: the Right-Left Rule
        
        
        Start at the identifier. Read right while you see [] or (); when you hit ) or the end, read left through * and qualifiers; when you hit (, step out of the parentheses and repeat. A qualifier binds to whatever is immediately to its left (or right, if nothing is on its left). Step through the curated set, type your own, or test yourself in quiz mode.
