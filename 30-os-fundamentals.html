// 30.3 — OS LAB 2 · PAGING, TLB & PAGE REPLACEMENT

Address Translation Stepper and Page-Replacement Race

Part A walks one virtual address at a time through the MMU (Memory Management Unit): split it into page number and offset, look in the TLB (Translation Lookaside Buffer, a tiny cache of recent translations), walk the page table on a miss, take a page fault if the page is not in RAM, pick a victim frame, and build the physical address. Part B races FIFO, LRU, OPT and Clock on one reference string.
